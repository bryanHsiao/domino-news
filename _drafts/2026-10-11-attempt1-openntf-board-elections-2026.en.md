OpenNTF has recently announced the results of its 2026-2027 Board elections, reflecting increased community engagement.

**Election Results**

**Member Directors (Two-Year Term):**

1. Serdar Basegmez - Developi Information Systems (Returning)
2. Jesse Gallagher - I Know Some Guys (Returning)
3. Heiko Voigt - Harbour Light / SIT (Returning)
4. Kim Greene - Kim Greene Consulting (Returning)
5. David Mulvey - Oval Business Solutions (New)

**Contributor Directors (One-Year Term):**

1. Christoph Adler - Panagenda (Returning)
2. David Hablewitz - Divergent Solutions (Returning)
3. Adam Foster - Oval Business Solutions (New)

This election marks the first in 12 years for OpenNTF, indicating a rise in community participation. We extend our gratitude to all candidates and welcome the new directors. Additionally, we thank outgoing directors Doug Robinson and Graham Acres for their contributions to the community.

We encourage all members to continue engaging with the community through our Discord server, projects, or webinars. For more information, please refer to the [official OpenNTF announcement](https://openntf.org/Internal/home.nsf/home.xsp).
