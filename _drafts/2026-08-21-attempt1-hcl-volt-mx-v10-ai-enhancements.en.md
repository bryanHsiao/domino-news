## HCL Volt MX v10: AI-Driven Innovations and Enhancements

HCL Volt MX v10 is the latest release of HCLSoftware's multiexperience low-code development platform, introducing several AI-driven features aimed at enhancing development efficiency and application intelligence.

### Key AI Enhancements

- **RAG in Volt IQ**: This feature leverages AI technology to provide more intelligent data processing and content generation capabilities through retrieval-augmented generation methods, enabling developers to build high-quality applications more rapidly. ([hcltech.com](https://www.hcltech.com/en-us/hcl-annual-report-2026/hcl-software?utm_source=openai))

- **Figma-to-Iris Design Import**: Developers can now directly import designs from Figma into Volt MX's Iris, facilitating seamless integration between design and development phases and reducing development timelines. ([hcltech.com](https://www.hcltech.com/en-us/hcl-annual-report-2026/hcl-software?utm_source=openai))

- **Car App Support**: Volt MX v10 extends support for car applications, allowing developers to create and deploy apps tailored for in-vehicle devices, addressing the growing demand in the automotive market. ([hcltech.com](https://www.hcltech.com/en-us/hcl-annual-report-2026/hcl-software?utm_source=openai))

- **Built-in Governance in Volt Foundry**: The new built-in governance feature offers enhanced control and oversight, ensuring security and compliance throughout the application development and deployment processes. ([hcltech.com](https://www.hcltech.com/en-us/hcl-annual-report-2026/hcl-software?utm_source=openai))

### Agentic AI Integration

HCL Volt MX v10 also introduces Agentic AI capabilities, an advanced AI technology designed to elevate application intelligence. With Agentic AI, developers can achieve more efficient app development and deliver superior user experiences. ([developer.ds.hcl-software.com](https://developer.ds.hcl-software.com/t/build-smarter-apps-with-agentic-ai/172430?utm_source=openai))

### Training and Certification

To assist developers in mastering the new features of Volt MX v10, HCLSoftware offers various training and certification programs. For instance, the HCL Volt MX Foundations Training and Certification held in April 2026 aims to enhance developers' skills, ensuring they can fully utilize the new version's capabilities. ([hclsoftwareu.hcl-software.com](https://hclsoftwareu.hcl-software.com/calendar-view/hcl-volt-mx/hcl-volt-mx-foundations-training-and-certification-04-13-2026/721?utm_source=openai))

In summary, HCL Volt MX v10 introduces a suite of AI-driven features and enhancements, providing developers with powerful tools to achieve greater success in multiexperience application development.
